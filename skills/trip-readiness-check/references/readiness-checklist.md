# Readiness checklist

Check only what is relevant to this trip. Not every category applies to every trip, and working through all of them on a two-night city break produces noise rather than a readiness verdict.

For each area, name the trip section you looked at, so "not recorded" is a statement about a specific place in the record rather than a general impression.

1. **Dates and destination.** `dates` and `destinations` are coherent, with no date contradiction.
2. **Flights and long-distance transport.** `flights` records an arrival and a departure, or the trip explicitly does not need them. Route and timing fit the constraints in the profile.
3. **Accommodation.** `accommodation` covers every required night, and the location and setup suit what the trip is for. An uncovered night is the highest-value finding in this list.
4. **Ground transport.** `transportation` covers airport or station transfers, a rental car, transit, or accessibility needs, where any of those are relevant.
5. **Confirmed bookings.** `confirmed_bookings` records the key reservations, with no duplicate or conflicting booking.
6. **Documents.** `documents` holds the confirmations, and the entry, visa, insurance or ticket detail, where those are relevant.
7. **Deadlines.** `deadlines` covers cancellation, payment, check-in, visa, reservation and event dates. A deadline that has already passed is urgent, not a gap.
8. **Food and reservations.** `restaurants_food`, only where advance booking or a dietary need makes it matter.
9. **Activities and events.** `activities` and `itinerary`, for a must-do or ticketed item with a timing dependency.
10. **Itinerary.** `itinerary` has enough structure to reveal an impossible overlap, an uncovered night, or an obvious transit gap.
11. **Traveler-specific needs.** The profile's work travel, family, pet, accessibility and loyalty sections, where they create a requirement for this trip.

Every finding is a statement about the record. "`accommodation` records nothing for the night of the 14th" is checkable. "You have not booked the 14th" is a guess about the world.
